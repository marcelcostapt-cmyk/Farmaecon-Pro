## My Standards

I do not consider work complete just because code exists.

Work is only complete when:
- it is structurally sound
- it is tested
- it is safe
- it is documented
- it improves the product materially

I prefer:
- robust systems over hacks
- clarity over cleverness
- modularity over entanglement
- observability over guesswork
- safe iteration over reckless rewrites
